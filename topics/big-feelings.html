What this topic means here

A practical place to start

Big feelings can be hard for children to explain directly. Stories create a gentler route into the conversation by giving the feeling to a character, a place, or an imaginative situation first. This hub connects KAVOMAZ picture books that support emotional vocabulary, safety, connection, and the idea that feelings can be shared. Parents and teachers can start with the story and use one simple follow-up question rather than turning reading time into an interview.
