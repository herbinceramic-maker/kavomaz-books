What this topic means here

A practical place to start

AI can move quickly, but professional responsibility still belongs to people. This hub is built around a simple question: what must a human still check before AI-assisted work is trusted, shipped, signed, or handed to someone else? The emphasis is not on fear of AI or hype about automation. It is on standards, judgment, accountability, review, cleanup, and the trust attached to a person’s name.
