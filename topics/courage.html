What this topic means here

A practical place to start

Courage in a children’s story does not need to mean fearlessness. It can mean taking one supported step while still feeling unsure. This hub centers that more realistic version of bravery and connects it to confidence, practice, and asking for help when needed. The emphasis stays gentle: children do not need to be pushed into a “brave” performance for the story to be useful.
