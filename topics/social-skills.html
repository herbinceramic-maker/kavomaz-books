What this topic means here

A practical place to start

Social confidence grows through small repeatable skills, not through performing a different personality. This hub is for readers who want help starting conversations, joining a group without interrupting, handling silence, saying no, making small talk feel less fake, or communicating clearly at work. The focus is useful communication, boundaries, repair, and confidence that comes from knowing what to do next.
