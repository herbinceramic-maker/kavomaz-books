What this topic means here

A practical place to start

Teamwork is more than asking children to share nicely. It includes waiting, taking a role, asking for help, repairing a misunderstanding, and noticing that different people can contribute in different ways. This hub uses story and practical guides to make those ideas visible. The aim is not perfect harmony; it is helping children experience what repair and cooperation look like when a group has a real goal.
