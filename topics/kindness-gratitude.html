What this topic means here

A practical place to start

Kindness and gratitude become more meaningful when children can see them in actions rather than only hear abstract instructions. Holiday stories can provide a natural setting for noticing help, saying thank you, repairing a small mistake, or recognizing what someone else contributed. The goal is not forced gratitude or perfect manners. It is to help children notice relationships, effort, and opportunities to respond with care.
