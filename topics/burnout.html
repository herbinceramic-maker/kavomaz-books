What this topic means here

A practical place to start

Burnout can look like exhaustion, irritability, numbness, avoidance, or the feeling that ordinary tasks suddenly require too much effort. Helpful reading should not reduce that experience to “try harder” advice. This hub brings together KAVOMAZ material that treats exhaustion as information and focuses on realistic recovery, boundaries, rest, and reducing unnecessary pressure. The central book offers a longer framework. Related guides answer narrower questions such as why rest alone may not feel restorative and how to protect energy when demands keep arriving.
