What this topic means here

A practical place to start

For young children, growth mindset is easier to understand through a story than through a slogan. A crooked fold or an imperfect nest can give a child enough distance to talk about disappointment without feeling corrected. This hub gathers KAVOMAZ stories where trying again is part of the plot rather than a lesson pasted on top. The goal is to make room for frustration, flexibility, learning, and another attempt.
