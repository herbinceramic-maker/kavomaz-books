About this book

Use AI aggressively. Keep the judgment human.

VerificationOwnershipPrivacyAI disclosure

This book is a practical standard for responsible AI-assisted work. It focuses on what still belongs to the professional after AI has helped: understanding the output, checking consequential claims, preserving uncertainty, protecting information, making the decision, and owning what leaves your hands.

It introduces practical ideas such as the Next-Person Test, the Reputation Tax, the OWN IT Gate, and the “last human pass” itself: the final review between machine assistance and a real-world handoff.

Available on Amazon in Kindle/eBook and paperback editions.

Core standard

AI can move fast. Your standard still has to hold.

JudgmentDo not outsource the decision

AI can draft, summarize and challenge your thinking. The professional still decides what matters and what is ready.

HandoffProtect the next person’s time

Productivity is not real if the recipient has to reconstruct your context, verify your claims or finish the thinking you were responsible for.

AccountabilityOwn what carries your name

Read it, understand it, verify what matters, make uncertainty visible and stand behind the final result.
