About this book

Small, practical people skills you can actually use

Starting conversationsAwkward momentsBoundariesWork & digital communication

This low-pressure guide treats social confidence as a set of learnable skills rather than a personality type. Its 48 short skills cover starting and continuing conversations, recovering from awkward moments, ending interactions kindly, setting boundaries, building everyday confidence, and communicating more clearly at work and online.

Each skill is designed to be used like a tool: understand why it matters, notice the common trap, try a practical alternative, and practice one small action in real life.

Available on Amazon in Kindle/eBook and paperback editions.

What readers can practice

ConversationStart and keep conversations moving

Use simple openings, easy open-ended questions, balanced sharing and natural follow-ups.
