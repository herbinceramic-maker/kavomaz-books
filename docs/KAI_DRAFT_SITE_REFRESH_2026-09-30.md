# KAI Draft Site Refresh — Books

Status: DRAFT ONLY — do not merge without owner approval.

## Evidence
- GSC (last 28 settled days through 2026-09-27): 12 impressions, 1 click, avg position 11.6.
- Homepage recent page-level position is strong on a tiny sample (around 3.6).
- Kids article pages have already appeared in positions 1–5.5 on very small samples.

## Copy audit
1. Homepage positioning is thoughtful but text-heavy above the fold.
2. “Practical / gentle / reader-first” concepts recur often across homepage sections.
3. The Life imprint dominates the homepage; Kids is clear but visually secondary.
4. “Start with the problem your reader already feels” is strong, but the following paragraph can be shorter.
5. Popular-links block is useful for SEO but visually reads like a long utility list.

## Draft direction
- Keep the trustworthy, non-hype tone.
- Shorten hero body and move explanatory copy lower.
- Give Life and Kids a clearer two-path visual choice near the top.
- Convert the long popular-links block into grouped topic clusters.
- Surface free-sample CTA more prominently without adding urgency.

## Visual draft direction
- Calm editorial motion: page-turn / paper-depth cues, not literal flashy animation.
- Optional subtle SVG/Lottie illustration for the Life/Kids split.
- Gentle reveal of book cards and reading guides.
- No motion that distracts from reading.
